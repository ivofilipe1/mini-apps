mini-apps

Small, practical tools for wellbeing, health, and the admin of everyday life.

Health and wellbeing

  • Tide Open→

    A paced breathing timer. Cyclic breaths, an open-ended hold on empty lungs, then a timed recovery hold. The screen fills and drains with your lungs, so the phase is readable without looking at the numbers.

    breathingno storageaudio + haptics
  • Trajectory Open→

    A weight and calorie tracker. Smooths your scale readings, fits your real energy expenditure from your own logs rather than a formula, and projects ninety days forward.

    healthlocal storage onlyhand-drawn svg chart
  • HeartQuake Open→

    Six bodyweight routines, each a fixed fifteen minutes: five of warm-up, ten of work. Compound exercises only, nothing to buy, and it remembers which ones you can't do.

    exerciselocal storage onlyaudio + haptics

Dutch learning

  • Wandeling Open→

    Dutch practice by conversation. A walk through ninety fixed everyday scenes: you answer in Dutch, the words you pick up come back as spaced repetition, and the Dutch is read aloud by your device.

    languagelocal storage onlynoncommercial licence
  • Ritme Open→

    Dutch grammar, rule first. A short card per topic, then quick runs of twelve: word order, verb forms, de/het and listening to casual speech. Misses come back until they stick. The companion to Wandeling.

    languagelocal storage onlyaudio

Why this exists

Tools I wanted, customizable, simple, local only. Most apps for health, habits and daily admin want an account, a subscription, and a copy of your data. These want none of that: each one is a single page that runs in your browser and keeps everything on your device.

These apps are not meant to store information for long term, but to enable quick habits on the go, with the ability to track progress on short term.
