mini-apps

Small, self-contained web apps. One HTML file each — open it in a browser and it runs.

  • Tide

    A paced breathing timer. Cyclic breaths, an open-ended hold on empty lungs, then a timed recovery hold. The screen fills and drains with your lungs, so the phase is readable without looking at the numbers.

    breathingno storageaudio + haptics
  • Trajectory

    A weight and calorie tracker. Smooths your scale readings, fits your real energy expenditure from your own logs rather than a formula, and projects ninety days forward.

    healthlocal storage onlyhand-drawn svg chart

House rules

  1. One file. No build step, no package manager, no framework. What you read is what runs.
  2. No outbound requests. No CDN, no webfont, no analytics, no tracking pixel. Type comes from fonts already on your device.
  3. Your data stays yours. Anything an app stores is kept in your own browser and never leaves it. There are no accounts and no server.
